SALES + FINANCING

Keep the conversation simple.

Use TITUS for the numbers. Use the presentation to explain value. Use Jobber for the official quote and financing.

01

Build the paver price

Measure front and back, select only the paver upgrades the customer wants, and let TITUS apply the paver minimum and approved bundle discount.

02

Use positioning correctly

The Positioning screen is paver-only: Chuck in the Truck, Revive, and Top-Dollar Todd. Exterior services never affect those paver comparison numbers. The nicknames are internal only.

03

Offer exterior add-ons

Ask what else the homeowner wants improved while Revive is already there: house wash, roof wash, driveway cleaning, gutters, cage cleaning, drainage, stain treatment, or another approved service.

04

Do not double-count packages

If a Revive package already includes a service, do not add the same service again. Add individual lines only for genuinely additional scope.

05

Present one final price

Do not walk the customer through every calculator line. Confirm the complete scope and confidently present the final selling price for everything selected.

06

Financing language

“We do offer financing through Wisetack right inside the Jobber quote. Wisetack will show you the personalized options available to you. I don’t want to guess at your payment because the terms are personalized.”

07

Discount guardrail

The paver bundle discount applies only when both front and back paver areas are included. Standard options are 0%, 5%, or 10%. 12%, 15%, and 20% require manager approval. Exterior services are not automatically discounted.

08

Paver minimum

The $1,199 minimum applies only to paver restoration/sealing base work. House washing, roof washing, pressure washing, gutters, drainage, and other exterior work use their own pricing.

09

Financing guardrail

Do not promise approval, a specific APR, a specific monthly payment, or a 0% option. The customer reviews their own offer inside Jobber.
