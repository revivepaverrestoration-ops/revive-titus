SALES + FINANCING

Keep the conversation simple.

Use TITUS for the numbers. Use your presentation and Jobber for the customer experience.

01

Bundle discount

Use only when both front and back are being completed. Apply 0%, 5%, or 10% to the combined base service only. Specialty upgrades stay full price.

02

Present the price

Do not walk the customer through every calculator line. Confirm the scope, present the final project price confidently, and explain the value behind the process.

03

Financing language

“We do offer financing through Wisetack right inside the Jobber quote. Wisetack will show you the personalized options available to you. I don’t want to guess at your payment because the terms are personalized.”

04

Financing guardrail

Do not promise approval, a specific APR, a specific monthly payment, or a 0% option. The customer reviews their own offer inside Jobber.

05

Price positioning

Compare preparation, joint work, sealing system, written warranty, aftercare, and verified credentials. Do not make unsupported claims about another contractor.

06

Minimum project

TITUS enforces the current $1,199 minimum. Do not reduce below the company minimum unless management approves the exception.
