SALES + FINANCING

Keep the conversation simple.

Use TITUS for the numbers. Use your presentation and Jobber for the customer experience.

01

Bundle discount

Use only when both front and back are being completed. Standard options are 0%, 5%, or 10%. Discounts of 12%, 15%, or 20% require manager approval. Specialty upgrades stay full price.

02

Present the price

Do not walk the customer through every calculator line. Confirm the scope, present the final project price confidently, and explain the value behind the process.

03

Financing language

“We do offer financing through Wisetack right inside the Jobber quote. Wisetack will show you the personalized options available to you. I don’t want to guess at your payment because the terms are personalized.”

04

Financing guardrail

Do not promise approval, a specific APR, a specific monthly payment, or a 0% option. The customer reviews their own offer inside Jobber.

05

Price positioning

Compare preparation, joint work, sealing system, written warranty, aftercare, and verified credentials. Do not make unsupported claims about another contractor.

06

Paver minimum

The $1,199 minimum applies to paver restoration/sealing base work. Exterior cleaning and add-ons use the service guide or approved project price.

07

On-site upsell

Ask what else the homeowner wants improved while Revive is already there. House wash, roof wash, gutters, cage cleaning, drainage, and stain treatment can be added without turning the conversation into a hard sell.

08

Package guardrail

Do not stack a saved package with the same individual services already included in that package. Add individual lines only for genuinely additional scope.
